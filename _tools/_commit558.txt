  COttoBlockManager::ProcessBlocks(), its intra-bar twin UpdateIntraBar(), and
  COttoOrderManager::PlaceOrArmOrder() each carry a new paragraph recording the
  consequence of the far-edge anchor: because the entry sits 0.5*ATR beyond the
  edge the target is anchored to, the reward-to-risk measured from the ENTRY is
  InpMaxRR - (0.5*ATR / slDist), not InpMaxRR. Worked case: a 1.0-high zone at
  ATR 2.0 with InpMaxRR 4.0 -> 3.5R. The note closes with "Do NOT fix it by
  anchoring the target on the entry", so a future maintainer meets the intent
  before the code. No line, offset or geometry changed.
