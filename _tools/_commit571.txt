571: fix — key the High Table aggregate ceiling to the worst rung (4.125% -> 1.50%)

RATIONALE

  v5.63 added an aggregate leg to AuditRiskExposure(): it sums the loss-side
  percentage of every leg behind the shared stop and compares that sum against
  the "stacked design budget" -- RiskPercent + InpRiskT2Pct + InpRiskT3Pct +
  InpRiskT4Pct, times the same 1.50 the per-leg check uses. On the shipped
  inputs that resolves to 2.75% nominal / 4.125% with tolerance, and both v5.63
  and v5.68 documented it as the pyramid's design ceiling, which "a
  correctly-sized ladder sits exactly at" and therefore "never alerts".

  That is false, and this release corrects the bound rather than the prose.

  1. THE 4.125% BOUND WAS UNREACHABLE.

     Every rung is opened roughly 1R from ONE shared stop, and ApplyUnifiedSL()
     ratchets that stop forward one way only (it refuses any looser value):

         T2 at +1.0R  ->  stop at breakeven    (primary entry +/- friction)
         T3 at +2.0R  ->  stop at primary +1.0R
         T4 at +3.0R  ->  stop at primary +2.0R

     The aggregate leg skips any leg whose loss-side distance is <= 0
     ("if(lossDist <= 0.0) continue;"). By the time T3 exists, T1 and T2 are
     both BEHIND the stop and contribute nothing; by T4, three legs contribute
     nothing. A healthy ladder therefore sums to roughly its NEWEST rung only:

         ~1.0% at T2      ~0.5% at T3      ~0.25% at T4

     never 2.75%. The ceiling could not fire on the stacking it was documented
     to catch, and the only way to reach it was a sizing error large enough
     that the per-leg check had already reported it. A guard that is
     unreachable through the geometry it guards is not an upper bound; it is
     dead weight that reads as coverage.

  2. THE BOUND IS NOW THE LARGEST RUNG.

         double maxRungRiskPct   = MathMax(InpRiskT2Pct, InpRiskT3Pct);
         maxRungRiskPct          = MathMax(maxRungRiskPct, InpRiskT4Pct);
         maxRungRiskPct          = MathMax(maxRungRiskPct, RiskPercent);
         double maxAllowedAggPct = maxRungRiskPct * 1.50;   // 1.50% shipped

     nominalStackPct is REMOVED, not kept beside the new bound: it has no
     remaining reader and an unused MQL5 local is a build warning.

  3. THE v5.70 PROPERTY SURVIVES.

     RiskPercent is folded into the max, so a zeroed or inverted
     InpRiskT2Pct/T3/T4 still cannot resolve the ceiling below the primary
     rung. A plain single-leg basket keeps exactly the 1.50% bound v5.70
     intended for it, and cannot trip this leg before any scale-in. The floor
     was never the part that was wrong; the SUM was.

  4. THIS REVERSES A DOCUMENTED DECISION, DELIBERATELY.

     _commit570.txt:37-43 rejected "dropping it to a single-rung ceiling" on
     the grounds that it "would alert WARN on every correctly-sized T1+T2
     basket, converting a targeted detector into constant noise", and stated
     that such a ladder "sits exactly at" 4.125%.

     Measured against the ratchet above, a correctly-sized T1+T2 basket reads
     ~1.0% against a 1.50% ceiling: 33% headroom, no alert. The rejection
     reasoned about the sum as if every leg were still exposed, which the
     shared stop prevents. It was recorded as a design claim rather than an
     observation, and it is wrong.

  5. WHAT DOES NOT MOVE.

     No entry, no exit, no sizing, no position count, no stop level. The leg
     keeps its own latch (m_alertSent_AggRiskBreach), its two-cycle
     confirmation (m_aggRiskSeen), its ClearLatch re-arm on the healthy path,
     and HT_SEV_WARN. Only the bound and the wording that explains it change.
     The alert text no longer blames "how many of them are stacked behind the
     shared stop" -- under the ratchet that condition is normal and healthy.
     It now reports the actual failure: the ratchet is NOT absorbing the legs
     behind the newest one.

  6. JOURNAL: DEEP FRONT-EDGE WORDING.

     COttoJournal still captioned the entry as a "Front-Run" in six
     operator-facing literals, while the shipped model has been the deep
     front-edge anticipation entry since v5.66/v5.69 ("TRUE DEEP FRONT-EDGE
     (v5.69)" in the ENTRY_FRONT_EDGE banner). The literals move onto the
     tree's own canonical vocabulary:

       * phase label    "C (Pure Trend Continuation)"
                     -> "C (Deep Front-Edge Anticipation)"
       * LogSetupArmed  "SUPPORT (Front-Run Breakdown / Sell at Near Edge
                         + 2.0*H + 1.5*ATR | SL 1R above entry)"
                     -> "SUPPORT (Deep Front-Edge Anticipation / Sell from
                         Near Edge + 2.0*H + 1.5*ATR | SL 1R above entry)"
       * the same for the RESISTANCE / Buy caption, and for the second copy
         of both pairs in LogEntry (four captions in total)
       * armed header   "(Front-Run: near edge +/- 2.0*H +/- 1.5*ATR)"
                     -> "(Deep Front-Edge: near edge +/- 2.0*H +/- 1.5*ATR)"

     The "Front-Run veto" wording is RETAINED on purpose, in the journal and
     everywhere else: the veto is still a front-run veto and
     InpEnableFrontRunVeto still names it. The retired phrases were the ones
     describing the ENTRY, which has not been a front-run since v5.66.

     CAVEAT: no release sketch for this wording was found on disk (searched
     *.txt, *.md, *.pine, *.json under Downloads). These strings are composed
     from the vocabulary already shipped in OttoDefines.mqh rather than copied
     from an operator spec. They are cosmetic -- printed text only, no parsed
     consumer -- but if a specific wording was intended, only the literals
     change; the ceiling work is independent of it.

PROBE IMPACT

  Retargeted, not weakened. Every changed pin keeps its original purpose:

    * test_v563_trail_health.py: the "sum of four rungs" and "floored sum"
      pins become the worst-rung pins, PLUS a new negative pin
      ("nominalStackPct" not in RISK_C) so the unreachable sum cannot quietly
      return. The comparison pin follows maxAllowedAggPct.
    * _mutate_v563.py: the three aggregate mutations were anchored on the
      removed symbols and would have reported SKIPPED -- which fails the run by
      design. They are retargeted onto the new bound: reverting it to the
      summed ladder, dropping the 1.50 tolerance, and dropping the RiskPercent
      term. Still 39 mutations, 0 skipped.
    * test_v557_frontrun.py: journal captions follow the new literals, plus two
      new pins -- the retired "Front-Run Breakdown/Breakout" phrases are gone,
      and "Front-Run veto anchor" SURVIVES (guarding against over-reach in
      exactly the direction this commit was tempted to go).
    * test_v540_hybrid.py: the constant-logic pin names the new phase string and
      asserts the old one is absent.

  All pins run against comment-stripped source, so prose describing the old
  layout can neither satisfy nor break them.
