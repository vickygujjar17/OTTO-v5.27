562: feat — split the milestone ladder from the ATR trail gate, arm the trail at +2.0R

RATIONALE

  v5.61 left the milestone ladder wired in series with the ATR trail: the single
  gate `if(currentRR >= InpTrailStartRR && !trancheOpenedThisTick)` did not just
  arm the ATR formula, it was the ONLY place the computed basket stop was pushed
  by ApplyUnifiedSL(). Because the ladder's first rung is the +1.0R
  cost-covering breakeven, InpTrailStartRR was therefore the de facto activation
  R:R of the WHOLE ladder -- even though it is named and documented as the
  trail's input.

  That coupling made the input impossible to retune. Raising InpTrailStartRR to
  2.0 to delay the trail would have silenced the +1.0R breakeven floor as well,
  leaving a winner between +1.0R and +2.0R wearing its ORIGINAL -1.0R stop.

SOURCE

  COttoTradeManager.mqh -- Update(), hoist (1 site)
    The application of the ladder is lifted out of the trail gate into its own
    unconditional block:

      - if(currentRR >= InpTrailStartRR && !trancheOpenedThisTick)
      + if(!trancheOpenedThisTick)

    The ApplyUnifiedSL(desiredSL) call and the v5.15 SetActiveTradeSL() mirror
    move with it. `&& !trancheOpenedThisTick` is RETAINED: it is what stops a
    stop write racing the broker's confirmation of a ticket filled on that same
    tick -- the v5.14/v5.20 INVALID_STOPS / instant-stopped-out failure mode.

    This is safe by construction, not by luck. desiredSL is seeded from
    trade.currentTrailSL (the live stop) and, for a multi-tranche basket, from
    GetSessionSL(). ApplyUnifiedSL() is a strict one-way ratchet
    (`if(isLong && newSL <= m_sessionSL) return;` and its SHORT mirror), and
    m_sessionSL is seeded at InitBasket(). So on any tick below every milestone
    desiredSL EQUALS the stop already live and the ratchet returns early: zero
    broker writes. The hoist changes WHEN the stop may be pushed, never WHICH
    stop is chosen -- the in-branch rungs still each keep their own Inp*RR gate.

  COttoTradeManager.mqh -- ATR formula gates kept (2 sites, unchanged text)
    Both `if(currentRR >= InpTrailStartRR)` arms (LONG high0 - mult*atr, SHORT
    low0 + mult*atr) are untouched. They now govern the ATR FORMULA only; the
    value they raise into desiredSL is carried by the unconditional block.

  OttoDefines.mqh -- InpTrailStartRR 1.0 -> 2.0
  OttoDefines.mqh -- InpTrailStartRR re-documented as the ATR-trail-only input;
  the v5.29 InpLock3RRR note rewritten to say "ATR trail gates" and to record
  the split; the STEP PROFIT LOCK ladder note now states that every floor is
  applied unconditionally behind its own trigger.

  COttoTradeManager.mqh -- InpTrailStartRR now has exactly three live reads:
  the two ATR formulas above and the m_trailActivations tally. The tally is the
  third read precisely because a single-ticket ModifySL() inside `currentRR >=
  InpTrailStartRR` may have been driven by the ladder rather than the trail;
  counting those as trail activations would overstate the trail. Comment
  truth-ups at the tranche guard (:221-230), the LONG/SHORT trail arms
  (:280-286, :339-341), the T2/T3 notes (:365-370, :474-476), the single-ticket
  push (:559-563) and the SyncTradeState classifier (:609-616).

RISK LADDER (unchanged, v5.61 values restated for the record)

  T1 1.0% RiskPercent at market | T2 1.0% at +1.0R | T3 0.5% at +2.0R |
  T4 0.25% at +3.0R -- sum 2.75% of equity with all four legs live.
  SafetyMaxRiskPct (1.5%) remains a PER-LEG abort threshold, not a basket-sum
  cap; AuditRiskExposure() tracks peakRiskPct -- the worst single leg -- and on
  this ladder that peak is 1.0%.

UNIFIED-SL PROGRESSION (the ladder the hoist makes unconditional)

  below +1.0R            original -1.0R stop
  at    +1.0R            cost-covering breakeven (entry +/- beOffset)   [unchanged]
  at    +2.0R            stop -> +1.0R lock rung                        [unchanged]
  at    +3.0R            stop -> +2.0R lock rung                        [unchanged]
  from  +2.0R onward     ATR trail also armed, tighter-of-the-two wins  [CHANGED]

  The one behavioural delta is the ATR gap: between +1.0R and +2.0R the stop is
  carried by the milestone floors with NO ATR component. This is intentional --
  a wide-ATR chop in that band can no longer pull the stop in behind the +1.0R
  floor. Above +2.0R the trail behaves exactly as it did from +1.0R in v5.61.
