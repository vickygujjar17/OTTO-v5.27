561: feat — update pyramiding allocation to 1/1/0.5/0.25 and unified trail ladder

The pyramid shipped a HALVING ladder of vestigial size (T2 0.25%, T3 0.125%,
T4 0.0625%) whose scale-in legs were protected by a floor that grew LOOSER the
further the basket ran. This change flattens the head of the ladder, quarters the
tail, and re-derives every scale-in floor from the unified-SL milestone the
basket is ALREADY wearing.

RISK LADDER 1.0 / 1.0 / 0.5 / 0.25 (OttoDefines.mqh)

  T1 stays at 1.0% (RiskPercent, group [6]) and is UNTOUCHED. The three scale-in
  inputs move:

    InpRiskT2Pct   0.25   -> 1.0
    InpRiskT3Pct   0.125  -> 0.5
    InpRiskT4Pct   0.0625 -> 0.25

  The four rungs therefore sum to 2.75% of equity with all four legs live,
  replacing the 0.6875% the old text claimed.

  SUMMED VS PER-LEG: RATIONALE. The comment that stood above these inputs said
  the four rungs "total 1.4375% ... comfortably inside the SafetyMaxRiskPct =
  1.5% budget". That sentence was doubly wrong and is now corrected in place.
  It was arithmetically wrong (the shipped defaults summed to 0.6875 once T1 read
  1.0, not the 1.4375 the text claimed -- the probe's own pin had drifted from
  the text), and it described a budget that nothing in the tree enforced:
  SafetyMaxRiskPct is read in exactly one place and has never been a
  basket-sum cap.

  What the runtime actually enforces is a PER-LEG threshold.
  CHighTableAuditor::AuditRiskExposure() tracks peakRiskPct -- the worst SINGLE
  leg, measured from its own entry to the SHARED basket SL -- and never the sum.
  leg is T1/T2 at 1.0%, and both of those legs read as risk-free (lossDist <= 0)
  once the unified SL ratchets past their entries, so the peak sits at 1.0%
  against the 1.5% budget. The auditor is therefore UNCHANGED by this release,
  and its pin (test_v546_risk_isolation.py) still passes 28/28.

  The 2.75% sum is deliberate, not an oversight: each rung is only reachable
  once the rungs beneath it already wear a milestone floor (T2 at friction
  breakeven, T3 at +1.0R, T4 at +2.0R), so the risk genuinely at stake is always
  a fraction of the nominal sum. The new comment states the sum explicitly and
  tells the reader NOT to read it against SafetyMaxRiskPct.

UNIFIED-SL SCALE-IN FLOORS (COttoTradeManager.mqh)

  The ladder's T3 and T4 blocks used to protect their brand-new legs with
  CalcBasketFriction() breakeven -- entry +/- friction. At the rung where they
  fire, that floor was already far LOOSER than the stop the rest of the basket
  was wearing, so a fresh leg entered carrying more risk than every ticket
  beneath it. Both blocks now derive their floor from the SAME unified-SL
  milestone the ladder above applies to the basket:

    T3 (+2.0R) -> primaryEntry +/- 1.0 * rrUnit
    T4 (+3.0R) -> primaryEntry +/- 2.0 * rrUnit

  matching InpLockProfit2TargetRR (lock at +1.0R) and InpLockProfitTargetRR
  (lock at +2.0R) respectively. No new input was needed: those two locks already
  ARE these milestones, so OttoDefines needed no T-ladder change for them.

  CLAMPED, and this is load-bearing. Handing the bare milestone to the fill
  would be a silent no-op. The v5.32-hoisted half-risk rung pins the basket at
  entry -/+ 0.5R from the +1.0R breakeven onward, so a bare +1.0R override is
  LOOSER than the floor already live and ApplyUnifiedSL()'s one-way ratchet would
  refuse it -- leaving the new leg with whatever the fill handed it. Both blocks
  therefore take the tighter of the milestone and GetSessionSL():

    LONG : MathMax(milestoneRaw, sessionNow)
    SHORT: MathMin(milestoneRaw, sessionNow)

  with the clamp applied only when a session stop is live (> 0). The new leg is
  consequently born wearing exactly the stop the basket already has, and the ATR
  trail ratchets it forward on the next tick as before. Local names safeBE and
  safeBE4 are renamed t3Floor and t4Floor to match what they now hold, and the
  two LogGroupStop() strings say 'Executed (Unified SL +1.0R)' / '(+2.0R)'
  instead of '(Trail Pending)'.

  T2 is deliberately unchanged: at +1.0R the friction breakeven IS the milestone
  (that is where InpBreakEvenRR puts the basket), so groupBE is already the
  unified floor and still travels with the fill. InpBreakEvenRR and both lock
  handlers are untouched.
