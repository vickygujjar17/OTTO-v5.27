546: risk audit — loss-side isolation and adopted magic-0 legs

AuditRiskExposure() in CHighTableAuditor.mqh is the EA's independent position
risk audit: it reads the TERMINAL BOOK, never the order layer, so it measures
the risk that ACTUALLY exists rather than the risk the sizer believes it wrote.
Two blind spots in that measurement are closed here. Neither is visible to the
compiler gate -- each is a property of a formula, not of a type.

  1. LOSS-SIDE ONLY. The scan measured capital at risk with
     `MathAbs(entry - sl)`. That is direction-agnostic: it returns a POSITIVE
     distance whether the stop sits below entry (real loss) or above it (a stop
     the unified trail has ratcheted to break-even / into profit). So from the
     moment ApplyUnifiedSL ratcheted a stop to or past break-even, the auditor
     went on reporting `distPts * tickValue * vol` as capital at risk and
     dispatched "HighTable: position risk breach" against a leg that could no
     longer lose money -- a false CRITICAL, latched, from the loudest channel
     the EA owns. The distance is now the SIGNED loss-side distance
     (`isLong ? entry - sl : sl - entry`) and a leg whose stop is at or beyond
     break-even (`lossDist <= 0`) is skipped before any money is computed.

  2. ADOPTED LEG VISIBILITY. Every book read in this class is magic-scoped
     (`POSITION_MAGIC == m_magic`), which is correct for the desync test but
     also hid the adopted magic-0 primary from the risk audit entirely: the one
     leg the operator did NOT size was the one leg never checked. The filter now
     admits exactly that ticket -- a magic mismatch still skips unless
     `m_trackedAdopted` is set AND the ticket equals `m_trackedPrimary`. Keying
     on the ticket rather than on magic 0 alone keeps the blast radius at one
     position, so an unrelated manual or foreign-EA magic-0 leg still cannot be
     counted.

Unchanged on purpose: the 35% tolerance over RiskPercent, the money conversion,
the worst-leg peak tracking, the WARN severity, the alert text, and the
ClearLatch() re-arm on a clean cycle. This release narrows what COUNTS as risk;
it does not retune the threshold.
