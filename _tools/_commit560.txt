Every source change bumps the release stamp, so the two-minor bump is applied
here as its own pass, and the single pending source edit rides with it: the SAR
reversal fallback in COttoOrderManager.mqh re-spelled the stop-distance
arithmetic inline instead of sharing CalcSLDistance().

  _tools/_bump_version.py 5.60 reports the documented idempotent no-op ("5.60 is

SAR STOP DE-DUPLICATED (COttoOrderManager.mqh, OpenReversalPosition)

  The SAR reversal fallback computed its stop as 'targetBlock.blockHeight +
  0.5 * atr' -- the SAME quantity CalcSLDistance(block, atr) already returns
  ('return block.blockHeight + (0.5 * atr);', line 691). Every other entry path
  (PlaceOrArmOrder at line 785, CheckVirtualTriggers at line 2856) already calls
  the helper; only this fallback re-spelled the arithmetic. The two spellings
  agreed numerically, but a future edit to the helper would have diverged this
  stop ALONE -- and with it the whole reversal basket's 1R basis, its pyramid
  ladder, its breakeven and its trail. The fallback now calls CalcSLDistance()
  with the identical shift-1 ATR it already fetched. The change is confined to
  the fallback arm (guarded by 'if(targetBlock.localSL <= 0)'), so a block whose
  localSL is already set is untouched.

  SCOPE, stated exactly: this removes ONE inline spelling, on the STOP-DISTANCE
  path in the order manager. It is deliberately NOT a tree-wide sweep, because
  the other surviving spellings are load-bearing redundancy, not accidents:

    - COttoOrderManager.mqh:681 (CalcEntryPrice) and COttoBlockManager.mqh:168
      (BlockEntryPrice) are a PAIRED entry geometry. Each file's comment names
      the other as its twin and says the two 'must stay identical or the
      Front-Run veto would judge a different price from the order's' (block
      manager, lines 162-164). Folding either onto CalcSLDistance() would be an
      entry-geometry change under the banner of a de-dup.
    - COttoBlockManager.mqh:555 and :756 compute 'calcSLDist' inside the two
      front-run-veto passes (live ProcessBlocks and the intra-bar twin). Those
      are mirrored on purpose too -- the comments call them 'the identical
      arithmetic in COttoOrderManager::PlaceOrArmOrder' and 'Intra-bar twin of
      the ProcessBlocks() projection above' -- and they feed a separate tpRR
      projection, not the order's stop.

  The SAR fallback is the one place where the mirroring was accidental rather
  than declared: it sits in the same file as CalcSLDistance() and its two
  siblings there (PlaceOrArmOrder at line 785, CheckVirtualTriggers at line
  2856) already call the helper, so the inline spelling was a lone outlier
  against its own file's convention. The remaining 'blockHeight + 0.5*ATR' hits
  are prose in comment boxes.

HISTORICAL MARKERS AND THE BUMP GUARD

  The 5.58 release introduced five '// v5.58 — ...' annotations. Because the
  bump went 5.58 -> 5.60 and no pass ever bumped through 5.58 again, they were
  never restated, and the census scans for the bare literal and only exempts
  lines carrying 'FIX ('; left alone they would have reported STALE on the next
  bump and exited 1. All five are restated to the sanctioned historical marker
  'FIX (v5.58)' (precedent: 'FIX (v5.26)' in OttoDefines; the 557 -> 558 bump
  restated 16 survivors the same way, see _tools/_commit559.txt). The NEW de-dup
  note is the opposite case: it is an uncommitted '+' line that lands in 5.60,
  so it carries 'FIX (v5.60)', not 5.58 -- labelling it 5.58 would claim a
  release it was never in. Widths grow at most 4 columns ('// vX — ' -> 'FIX
  (vX) '), within the file's own accepted budget (existing FIX (v5.x) marker
  lines run to 86 columns). Both restatements and the relabel are applied by
  _tools/_annotate560.py.

  The guard is not argued, it is MEASURED: _tools/_bump_probe.ps1 runs the real
  next bump (5.60 -> 5.61), captures the verdict, then restores the tree
  byte-for-byte (SHA-256 verified identical). Before the relabel the probe
  reported '*** STALE v5.60 REMAINS *** COttoOrderManager.mqh:1487' and exited
  1; after it, 'no v5.60 literal remains in the shipped sources or docs',
  exit 0. That is what pinned the marker form.
